Nook Bench

Benchmarks for Nook's on-device model work. Inference runs on this device only. The one network use is downloading model weights from huggingface.co, and that only happens when you press a Download button below.

  1. Download the models you want to test (2B and 0.8B at least; Qwen3 1.7B for the stability baseline).
  2. Run the experiments, one at a time, top to bottom. Keep this tab open and the computer awake.
  3. Press Copy results at the bottom and paste the block back into the chat.
